🛡️ Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 November 2026  ·  Last updated: 25 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FarHaven (“the Bot”, “we”, “us” or “our”) may collect, use, store, and disclose information when you use the FarHaven Discord bot or interact with its features.

We aim to collect and retain only the information reasonably needed to operate, maintain, secure, and improve FarHaven. The exact information processed depends on which features a Discord server enables and uses.

📋 1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features enabled and how you interact with FarHaven, the Bot may process the following categories of information:

  • Discord identifiers: Discord user IDs, server (guild) IDs, channel IDs, and role IDs where required for a feature.
  • Server settings: configuration choices, enabled modules, command settings, and other preferences set by server administrators.
  • User preferences: settings or choices saved when a feature needs them to work.
  • Usage and diagnostic information: commands used, feature interactions, error reports, timestamps, and technical logs needed to maintain the Bot.
  • Submitted information: information you choose to provide through commands, forms, buttons, or other Bot features.
  • Message or interaction data: content or interaction details only where a feature requires access to them and the Bot has the relevant Discord permissions.

FarHaven does not ask users to provide account passwords, Discord authentication tokens, or payment-card details. Do not submit sensitive personal information through Bot commands.

⚙️ 2. How We Use Information

Information processed by FarHaven may be used to:

  • provide and operate requested Bot commands and features;
  • save and apply server configurations and user preferences;
  • respond to commands and process feature interactions;
  • maintain reliability, troubleshoot errors, and provide technical support;
  • detect spam, abuse, exploitation, or activity that threatens the Bot or its users;
  • protect the Bot, its infrastructure, and the Discord communities in which it operates; and
  • understand feature performance and improve the service.

💬 3. Message Content and Discord Permissions

FarHaven does not intentionally store all messages sent in a Discord server as a general message archive. A feature may temporarily process message content or related information when that is necessary to perform the feature requested or enabled by a server.

Features that require access to message content, member information, or other Discord data depend on the permissions and intents granted to the Bot and the applicable Discord platform requirements. Server administrators should review the Bot's requested permissions and enable only the features they intend to use.

🔒 4. Data Storage and Security

Information may be stored in databases, application logs, or other systems used to operate FarHaven. We aim to use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards to protect information against unauthorised access, alteration, loss, or disclosure.

No online service or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users and server administrators should also protect their Discord accounts and limit access to server administration permissions.

🤝 5. Data Sharing and Service Providers

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed or shared only as reasonably necessary in circumstances such as:

  • Infrastructure providers: hosting, database, monitoring, and other providers that help operate FarHaven;
  • Discord: information is exchanged with Discord as part of the platform and Bot functionality;
  • Legal requirements: where disclosure is required by applicable law or a valid legal process; or
  • Protection and safety: where reasonably necessary to investigate abuse, enforce applicable rules, or protect users, the service, or others.

Third-party providers may process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

🗑️ 6. Data Retention and Deletion

We aim to keep information only for as long as it is needed for the feature or purpose for which it was collected, for legitimate operational and security needs, or as required by law. Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the feature involved.

When information is no longer needed, we aim to delete it or remove identifying details within a reasonable period, subject to technical limitations, backups, and legal obligations.

Removing FarHaven from a server may stop further processing for that server, but it may not automatically erase information already stored. A deletion request may be needed.

🙋 7. Your Choices and Data Requests

Depending on applicable law and the circumstances, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of information associated with your Discord account or server.

To make a privacy request, contact us using the contact details below. Include your Discord user ID or server ID and a clear description of your request. Never send your password, authentication token, or other login credentials. We may ask for reasonable information to verify and process a request.

🧒 8. Children's Privacy

FarHaven is a general-purpose Discord bot and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children in circumstances where doing so would violate applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information to the Bot, please contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

🔗 9. Third-Party Services and Discord

FarHaven operates on Discord and may rely on third-party hosting, databases, APIs, or other technical services. Your use of Discord is also governed by Discord's own terms and privacy policy. We are not responsible for the independent privacy practices of third-party services.

📝 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as FarHaven's features, operations, or legal requirements change. The revised version will be published at this page with an updated “Last updated” date. Continued use of the Bot after an update means the updated policy will apply to future use, to the extent permitted by law.
